# 📊 AI Impact on Student Life Analysis

## 📌 Overview
This project analyzes how students across 15 cities use AI tools in their academic life, focusing on usage patterns, satisfaction levels, and grade outcomes.  
The dashboard was built in **Power BI** and provides interactive insights into the role of AI in education.

---

## 🗂 Dataset Highlights
- **Total Students:** 400  
- **Cities Covered:** 15  
- **AI Tools Analyzed:** 5  
- **High Satisfaction Students:** 149  
- **Average Daily Usage:** 2.93 hours  

---

## 📈 Key Insights
- **Academic Impact:**  
  - Improved grades: 139 students  
  - No change: 131 students  
  - Slight decline: 130 students  

- **Tool Popularity:**  
  - ChatGPT (380) and Gemini (365) dominate usage  
  - Copilot (306) shows strong adoption  
  - Grammarly (82) and Notion AI (40) are less used  

- **Usage by Age:**  
  - Highest usage among ages 22–24 (3.2–3.3 hrs/day)  

- **Purpose of Use:**  
  - Learning (26%), Homework (24%), Coding (22%), Research (22%), Writing (6%)  

- **Satisfaction by Gender:**  
  - Female: High 51.68%, Low 48.32%  
  - Male: High 50.70%, Low 49.30%
